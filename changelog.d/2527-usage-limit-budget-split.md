The usage-limit screen journal keeps a separate sighting budget per runtime launch for the bare phrases (`usage limit`, `weekly limit`) and the specific phrases, so quoted text on screen can no longer use up the budget a real limit hit needs. The `further usage-limit screen sightings suppressed` line carries a `marker_class` field, and the sighting and unmapped-record bounds are documented per runtime launch rather than per session.
