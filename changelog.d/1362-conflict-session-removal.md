`SessionInfo::can_stop()` and `can_remove()` are now true for a `conflict` runtime that names its worker and worker instance, because the daemon stops or removes such a session after proving its recorded identity (including `resume_binding_shape_mismatch`); they stay false for a conflict that names no worker, and the retention sweep still never selects a conflict. `session.remove_accepting_unconfirmed` (`session rm --accept-unconfirmed-cleanup`) also removes a conflict whose record names no worker generation, and the new `identity_proof_skipped` field of the result (omitted when false, shown by the CLI) says the proof was skipped; the daemon refuses that removal while a worker still answers on the session's slot or a journaled worker still runs. The helpers cannot see whether a generation is recorded, so a conflict that names a worker but no generation reports true and its stop is refused with `session_runtime_conflict`. `docs/public-api.md` and `docs/cli.md` describe the rules.
